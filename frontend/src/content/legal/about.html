About JazzGhost

Who We Are

JazzGhost is a technology platform that provides simple, accessible tools for processing publicly available media links. We build straightforward, reliable utilities that help people work with content they already have the right to access — quickly, and without unnecessary complexity.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a fast, transparent, and trustworthy technical tool that respects both user needs and the rights of content creators. We believe technology should be simple to use while remaining responsible and compliant with the platforms and laws that govern digital content.

The Problem We Solve

Publicly shared media is often difficult to save for legitimate personal use — such as archiving your own content, saving material you have permission to use, or accessing files for offline reference. JazzGhost offers a straightforward technical solution: a tool that processes a publicly available link and returns a downloadable version of the associated media, without unnecessary steps or software installation.

How It Works

Using JazzGhost is simple:

  1. You provide a publicly available URL from a supported platform
  2. Our tool technically processes the link
  3. You receive a downloadable version of the associated media, ready for personal use

JazzGhost does not host, store, or claim ownership of any third-party content. We are a technology tool — not a content library, publisher, or media distributor.

Our Commitments

User Privacy

We are committed to handling user data responsibly and transparently, in accordance with our Privacy Policy, and in compliance with applicable data protection laws such as GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA.

Responsible Technology

We design our tools to serve legitimate personal use cases and actively discourage misuse, including unauthorized access to private content or infringement of copyright.

Copyright Respect

We respect the intellectual property rights of creators and platforms. Our Terms of Service place clear responsibility on users to ensure they have the appropriate rights before downloading or using any content, and we maintain a copyright complaint process for rights holders.

Security

We apply reasonable technical safeguards, including working with reputable infrastructure and security partners such as Cloudflare, to help keep the Website safe, stable, and reliable.

A Tool, Not a Content Owner

It's important to us that our role is clearly understood: JazzGhost is strictly a technical intermediary tool. We do not host, store, stream, index, catalog, or make searchable any third-party media. Our tool performs a real-time technical conversion of a link the user supplies; it does not maintain a library, archive, or repository of content for retrieval by other users. We do not own, endorse, verify, or take responsibility for third-party content. We are not affiliated with the platforms our tool supports. Responsibility for ensuring lawful use — including securing any necessary rights or permissions before downloading or using content — rests entirely with the individual user, not with JazzGhost.

We believe transparency is the foundation of a trustworthy online tool.
