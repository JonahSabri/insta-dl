Contact Us

We welcome your questions, feedback, and requests. JazzGhost is committed to responding to legitimate inquiries in a timely and professional manner. Please review the categories below to help direct your message appropriately.

How to Reach Us

  • Email: contact@jazzghost.com
  • Address: JazzGhost is a digital-first, online-only service and does not maintain a public-facing physical office. All correspondence is handled exclusively through contact@jazzghost.com.

Reasons to Contact JazzGhost

Support Requests

If you're experiencing a technical issue, encountering an error, or have a general question about how to use our tools, please reach out to contact@jazzghost.com with a description of the issue, including the platform and URL type involved (without sharing any private or sensitive links).

Copyright Concerns

If you are a rights holder and believe content accessible through our Service infringes your copyright, please submit a notice in accordance with our copyright complaints process outlined in our Terms of Service. Include the specific URL, a description of the copyrighted work, and your contact details.

Privacy Requests

To exercise your privacy rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, or other applicable data protection laws — including requests to access, correct, delete, or restrict your personal data — please contact us at contact@jazzghost.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line. See our Privacy Policy for full details on your rights.

Business Inquiries

For partnership proposals, advertising inquiries, or other business-related matters, please contact contact@jazzghost.com with "Business Inquiry" in the subject line.

Legal Requests

For formal legal notices, law enforcement requests, or other legal matters, please contact contact@jazzghost.com. JazzGhost is a digital-first, online-only service and does not maintain a public-facing physical office for in-person correspondence.

Response Time

We aim to respond to all legitimate inquiries within a reasonable timeframe, typically within 3 business days. Response times may vary depending on the nature and complexity of the request.
